## The part

The OEM lids are a sealed panel. These have the vent cut through the lid
itself rather than louvred on top of it, so air can pass the headlight
housing while the outer shape stays the same as the part it replaces.

They fit the FD3S, 1993–2002 — USDM, JDM and EDM. The car is mirrored and
the lid is not, so left and right are separate files and a pair is a pair.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Size per side | 269.2 × 308.3 × 263.5 mm · 338.08 cm³ · 35,704 triangles |
| Material | PC-ABS or ASA. **Not PLA** — it sits over the headlight and in engine-bay heat. |
| Print | 0.4–0.6 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 5 perimeters, 25–30% infill |
| Finish | Sand, prime, paint. ASA and PC-ABS take automotive paint. |

**Check your bed first.** 269 and 308 mm both exceed a 256 mm bed, and the
part is over size on two axes — no rotation fixes it. You need a printer
that genuinely takes roughly 270 × 310 mm, or you split the model yourself
and join it. Splitting it for your own printer is fine: that is private use,
not a derivative you share.

## Before you print

**The one catch, said up front:** you either trim the thin alignment tab off
your headlight bezels, or you leave the bezels off. Neither is hard — but
this is not a bolt-on to a fully assembled stock bezel.

- The mounting tabs are what snap on the OEM covers. These are modelled
  thicker there — that's the point of printing your own. Don't thin them out
  to save time.

- Print one side first and test-fit before you commit a spool to the pair.

- If you scale anything, scale it by measured feature, not by eye — the
  mounting points are the tolerance that matters.
