Welcome to rx7.pro, basically just a small blog for rx7 related things and maybe some generic Mazda/MX5 things as well.

I'm a software engineer who has always been into Mazda's, it started with a Mazda 2 then I met my girlfriend who had a highly modified EP3 who then got me into cars. I got a Mazda 3 and sprayed a few things black then sold it due to it was a diesel, she recommended a MX5 as a good starting point for modification so of course I strapped a turbo to it!

Anyway my next project had to be something different so I had to get a RX7, but I didn't want too much of a project but being old and Japanese it turned out to be basically full restoration...

I'll be sharing some tips and tricks, 3d models and the overall journey
