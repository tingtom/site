WHAT THESE ARE
Vented headlight covers (lids) for the FD3S RX-7, 1993-2002. The OEM lids are a
sealed panel; these let air past the headlight housing. Same outer shape as the
lid, with the vent cut through.

FILES
  FD3S-headlight-cover-LEFT.stl    printable, driver side
  FD3S-headlight-cover-RIGHT.stl   printable, passenger side (mirror)
  FD3S-headlight-cover-LEFT.step   CAD, driver side
  FD3S-headlight-cover-RIGHT.step  CAD, passenger side

  The car is mirrored, the lid is not - left and right are separate files.

MEASURED SIZE (per side)
  269.2 x 308.3 x 263.5 mm
  338.08 cm3 of material
  35,704 triangles (the raised mark accounts for the extra triangles)

BED SIZE - READ THIS FIRST
  269 and 308 mm both exceed a 256 mm bed, and it is over on two axes, so no
  rotation fixes it. You need a printer that genuinely takes ~270 x 310 mm, or
  you split the part and join it. Splitting for your own printer is private use
  and is fine. Check your bed before you slice.

MATERIAL
  PC-ABS or ASA. Not PLA. The cover sits over the headlight and in the engine
  bay; PLA goes soft and sags well before the temperature the part sees.
  ASA/PC-ABS also take paint, which is how you finish them.

PRINT SETTINGS THAT WORKED
  0.4-0.6 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers
  5 perimeters, 25-30% infill
  The mounting tabs are the part that breaks on the OEM covers - do not skimp
  the walls there.

THE ONE CATCH, SAY IT UP FRONT
  You either trim the thin alignment tab off your headlight bezels, or you
  leave the bezels off. Neither is hard. It is not a bolt-on to a fully
  assembled stock bezel.
